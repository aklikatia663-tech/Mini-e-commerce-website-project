About Us

Our Story

Founded in Tizi Ouzou, Algeria, Drinks House began with a simple mission: to provide fresh, natural, and delicious beverages to our community.

We believe that what you drink matters. That's why we source only the finest ingredients - fresh fruits, premium tea leaves, and carefully selected coffee beans - to create drinks that not only taste amazing but also nourish your body.

From our refreshing fruit juices and creamy smoothies to our aromatic teas and expertly crafted coffees, every drink is prepared with care and passion.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, natural beverages that refresh, energize, and bring joy to every customer while promoting a healthy lifestyle.

👁️

Our Vision

To become Algeria's leading natural beverage brand, known for quality, innovation, and commitment to customer satisfaction.

💚

Our Values

Quality, sustainability, customer focus, and a passion for creating the best natural drinks using locally sourced ingredients.
