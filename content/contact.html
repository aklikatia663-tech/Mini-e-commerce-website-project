Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver across Algeria?

Yes! We deliver to all 48 wilayas. Delivery fees may vary based on your location.

How long does delivery take?

Within Tizi Ouzou: Same day or next day. Other wilayas: 2-4 business days.

Can I customize my drinks?

Absolutely! You can add special instructions when placing your order.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery (COD) and CCP/BaridiMob transfers.
